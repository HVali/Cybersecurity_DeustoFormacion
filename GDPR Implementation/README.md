### Technical and Organisational Security at AgroVerdeDeusto S.L.

AgroVerdeDeusto S.L. applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to guarantee the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the personal data processed, including:

- **Up-to-date antivirus:** All computers have the AgroShield Pro v5.2 antivirus software, with automatic daily updates and real-time protection against malware and unauthorised access.
- **Secure passwords, renewed periodically:** System users use alphanumeric passwords of at least 12 characters, renewed every 90 days. Two-factor authentication (2FA) is applied for remote access.
- **Automatic backups:** Encrypted backups are made daily with the GreenBackup Cloud system, with redundant storage on servers located in Spain. Backups are kept for 30 days.
- **Clean desk and physical access control:** A "clean desk" policy is applied in all offices. Paper documents containing personal data are kept in locked filing cabinets. Access to sensitive areas (servers, document archive) is restricted with ID cards and an entry log.
- **Use of corporate email for communications with personal data:** Communications containing personal data are sent exclusively through the corporate email (@agroverdedeusto.es), which is protected with TLS encryption and monitoring of suspicious activity.

### Internal Training at AgroVerdeDeusto S.L.

AgroVerdeDeusto S.L. holds annual training sessions for all staff, with the aim of strengthening the culture of data protection and digital security in the workplace. The sessions are held in person at the Valencia headquarters and are also available online through the internal virtual campus.

**Topics covered in the training:**

- **Good practices in handling personal data:** ▸ Identifying sensitive data ▸ Correct use of forms and consents ▸ Secure disposal of paper and digital documents ▸ Practical examples of common mistakes and how to avoid them
- **Digital security:** ▸ Use of secure passwords and two-factor authentication ▸ Detecting fraudulent emails (phishing) ▸ Protecting mobile devices and laptops ▸ Cyberattack drills and incident response
- **Internal procedures:** ▸ Information access protocols ▸ Management of incidents and security breaches ▸ Record of processing activities ▸ Secure internal communication between departments

The last training session took place on 12 March 2025, with 92% of the staff taking part. Training materials were handed out and a final assessment was carried out to check understanding of the content.

### Internal Procedure for Handling Rights Requests

#### 1. Receiving the request

- The data subject can exercise their rights by email, through a web form or in person.
- The request must be recorded in an **internal register**, stating the date, the type of right requested and the requester's details.

#### 2. Identity verification

- If the request does not allow the data subject to be identified, additional documentation will be requested (DNI, NIE, etc.).
- In the case of representation, a copy of the power of attorney or a signed authorisation will be requested.

#### 3. Assessment of the request

- It is checked whether the data processing is active and whether the requested right applies.
- The data controller or the data processor is consulted if necessary.

#### 4. Resolution and response

- The data subject receives a response within a maximum of **1 month** from receipt.
- If the request is complex, the deadline can be extended to **2 months**, informing the data subject.
- The response must be clear, free of charge and in plain language.

#### 5. Documentation and filing

- A copy of the request, the response and any related documentation is kept for at least **3 years**.
- The record of processing activities is updated if the processing has been modified or cancelled.

#### 6. Complaint channel

- The data subject is informed of their right to lodge a complaint with the **Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)** if they disagree with the response.

## Final Reflection on the GDPR Implementation

Data protection is not only a legal obligation, but an opportunity to:

- **Increase the trust** of clients and suppliers
- **Avoid penalties** and reputational risks
- **Optimise internal processes** through responsible information management
- **Show ethical commitment** in a technical and regulated sector such as plant protection products
